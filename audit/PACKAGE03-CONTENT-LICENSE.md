# Handbook-derived teaching content

The revised `complexity` lesson in `audit/package03-lessons/complexity.json` adapts chapter2 of **Competitive Programmer’s Handbook**, Antti Laaksonen, Draft July3,2018. The supplied chapter's substantive examples and data are retained; Mongolian/English wording is adapted, explanations and traces are expanded, and the C++ implementations/type choices are revised. It is not an official translation or author endorsement.
